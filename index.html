Cricket analytics Live

CricLens

A self-serve analytics platform that gives anyone the kind of cricket breakdown that used to live inside a team's back room.

Three workspaces — Match Lab, Player Lab and Team Lab — turn raw ball-by-ball data into something you can actually read: wagon wheels, scoring heat maps, batsman and bowler strength–weakness profiles, and team-combination analysis that shows which eleven holds up against which opposition.

AI operating system In development

Jatayu

A voice-first assistant built the way we actually want to work — one that listens, reasons across our tools, and acts.

Named for the Ramayana's watchful bird, Jatayu is a multi-agent system fronted by a Sri Yantra-inspired 3D interface. Specialised agents cover Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and messaging platforms, coordinated by a transport-agnostic core we call the Brain. A model router is in progress so each task lands on the model best suited to it.
